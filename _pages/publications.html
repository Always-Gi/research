Does a Larger Sample Size Always Lead to Shorter Confidence Intervals?

Jinwoong Park and Hanggi Cho*

Under review at Statistics & Probability Letters

*Corresponding author

We construct a counterexample showing that, within natural exponential families, the expected length of an exact equal-tailed confidence interval need not decrease monotonically as the sample size increases.
